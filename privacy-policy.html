Tai Chi Walking (הליכת טאי צ׳י) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: EDTech
Contact: edtech.isr@gmail.com

Tai Chi Walking is a daily movement practice app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.
  • The app has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information to us or to anyone else.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. It leaves only in the ways below, each of which you start yourself.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your questionnaire answers: an optional first name, age range, practice length, days per week, balance, knee condition, main goal, health considerations you chose to mark (for example high blood pressure or osteoporosis), and whether you want the brisk-walk card.
  • Your progress: which sessions you completed or skipped, and when.
  • Your monthly measurements: seconds on one leg and chair stands in 30 seconds.
  • Up to three automatic backups of the above, your sound setting and your language choice.

We never receive this data. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Where your data can go, and only if you choose

Android backup. If backup is on for your Google account, Android may include your progress and its automatic backups in your device backup and restore them on a new phone. This is a feature of Android and your Google account; Google's privacy policy applies. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Export. When you copy your backup to the clipboard, save it to a file, or share it, the app hands the text to the place you choose. That place's own terms apply.

Sharing the app. When you tap "Share the app", your phone's share menu opens with a short message and the app's Play Store link. You choose whether and where to send it. Nothing about you is included.

Exercise videos. Video links open YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app. From that point, YouTube's terms and privacy policy apply.

Health information

Some answers describe your health (for example osteoporosis or a recent joint replacement). They are used only on your phone, to adapt the exercises, and are never sent anywhere. The app gives general movement guidance; it does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If what the app collects or shares ever changes, this policy and its effective date will be updated before the change reaches the app.
